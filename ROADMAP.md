# 🗺️ Roadmap: the levels of this project

Think of the project like a game with levels. You finish one level, then the next one unlocks.

| Level | Name | What happens | Rough time | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research | Find out if the app is possible and how to build it | — | ✅ Done (Oct 31, 2026) |
| 2 | The Plan | Write the PRD: the blueprint that says what's in the first version and what comes later | 1–2 chats | ✅ Done (Oct 31, 2026) |
| 3 | Test build | One animated lesson screen, to prove the app runs smoothly on your PC | 1–3 chats | ✅ Done (Nov 1, 2026): you tried it and said the visuals are solid and the concept is perfect; the mascot and some choppiness are fix tasks (T45, T46) |
| 4 | First playable version | The real app: learning path, lessons, reviews, streaks. It's built one small piece per chat, band units first and tower-spotting units after. | about 1–3 months | 👉 You are here |
| 5 | Polish | Smoother animations, sounds, a mascot, more photos | a few weeks | 🔒 Locked |
| 6 | Keep it fresh | Every 3 months, check the band and carrier facts (they change!) and update the lessons | ongoing | 🔒 Locked |

The times are guesses. Claude does the coding; your time goes into testing and deciding, so you can go as fast or as slow as you like.

## The 10 lesson units planned for Level 4
1. **The spectrum ruler:** what bands are; low, mid and high
2. **Low band:** n71, B12, B13, B14 and friends
3. **Mid band:** n25, n66, B2, B4 and friends
4. **Capacity bands:** n41, n77, and the mmWave bands n258, n260, n261
5. **Who uses what:** which company uses which band
6. **Site types:** towers, rooftops, fake trees, small cells
7. **Reading a site:** stickers, signs, height rules
8. **Hardware:** antennas, radios, brands
9. **Cell IDs:** the numbers your phone sees
10. **Final challenge:** put all the clues together

The plan (Level 2) can change this list. That's what the plan is for.
