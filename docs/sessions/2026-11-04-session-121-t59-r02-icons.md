## In simple words (for the owner)
The app's buttons and labels used emoji (🔥 ⚡ 🔒 ⚙️ 🌡️ 🚗 📡). Emoji look different on every computer, so the app would have changed its look on your Mac or iPhone later. Now every one of them is a small picture Claude drew in code, all in the same style: a flame for the streak, lightning for XP, a little radio tower for the lesson you're on, a lock, a tick, a snowflake, a spanner, a gear, and so on. A test now fails if an emoji comes back.

## What was done
- **The icon set** (`app/src/theme/icons.ts`): 22 icons on a 24 × 24 grid, 2 px lines with round ends, one colour (they take the colour of the words around them). The 20 that `DESIGN.md` lists (flame, bolt, tower, lock, check, cross, waves, mapPin, repeat, grid, gear, star, freeze, repair, back, close, soundOn, soundOff, info, chevron) plus search and image. It's plain data, so tools can read it too.
- **`<Icon>` and `<SignalBars>`** (`app/src/Icon.tsx`): draw an icon (optionally filled, for earned stars) and phone-style signal bars (0 to 5).
- **Every screen switched over:**
  - Top of the path: the heatmap 🌡️ and settings ⚙️ buttons became round 44 × 44 icon buttons (grid, gear); 🔥 → flame, ⚡ → bolt, ✅ → tick, 🧊 → snowflake, 🛠 → spanner, 📢 → waves.
  - Path map: 🔒 → lock, ✓ → tick, 📡 → tower; 🚗 on the daily review → repeat; ⭐ → drawn stars.
  - Lessons: ✕ → close (now 44 × 44), "Right!" / "Not quite." get a tick / cross, scenario 🔎 → magnifier, ✅ / ❌ in the clue list → tick / cross with a hidden "Right:" / "Missed:" label, 🖼️ → picture frame, the match arrow → chevron.
  - Lesson complete: three stars always shown (earned ones filled, the rest dim), and drawn signal bars instead of the ▮▯ characters.
  - Heatmap and settings: ← Back → back arrow; Sounds shows a speaker that changes with the switch.
- **Tests:** `src/icons.test.ts` (no emoji or stand-in symbols in any `.tsx` file outside comments; the check really catches them; the set has every DESIGN.md icon; every icon stays inside its grid) and `tests/icons.spec.ts` (the main screen shows the right icons, the two icon buttons are at least 44 × 44, icons take the token colour, no emoji in the screen's text, close and back use icons). `tests/save.spec.ts` and `tests/types.spec.ts` no longer look for "🔥 1" and "✅".
- **Gallery:** a new `00-icons.webp` sheet (every icon at 48 and 24 px, plus the filled star). `node tools/gallery.mjs --icons-only` makes just that sheet.
- **Checks:** unit tests 113 pass (108 + 5 new); click tests 16 pass (15 + 1 new; frame-rate badge 60 fps); TypeScript OK; content checker OK (253 facts, 27 photos, 635 questions; `content/` untouched). The Windows build runs on the pull request.
