# 🎨 Style guide: the "Night Signal" look

**Status:** SIGNED OFF by the owner on 2026-11-04 (with `docs/redesign/PLAN.md`). Written 2026-11-04 in T58 (session 119).
**The 24 redesign sessions are done (2026-11-05, design review 3).** This guide stays the rulebook for every idea chat after them (`docs/redesign/IDEAS.md`).
**Picked by the owner on 2026-11-04:** look A "Night Signal", mascot "Beacon". The picture everyone copies from: `looks/look-a-night-signal.webp` (its source: `looks/looks.html`). Looks B and C were not picked; they stay in `looks/` for the record.

## In simple words (for the owner)
This is the app's rulebook for how things look, sound and move, like a team's kit rules: same colours, same letters, same way of moving on every screen. Every redesign chat follows it, so the app feels like one finished thing instead of a patchwork. The feeling we're after: **dark, calm and glowing, like a radio engineer's screen at night.** Lessons light up like towers "coming on air".

---

## 1. Feel (what every screen should say)
- **Calm, not loud.** Dark background, a few glowing colours. Glow means "alive" or "on air".
- **Radio, not cartoon.** Rings, waves, signal lines, meters. Original to us: nothing from Duolingo (decision of 2026-10-31).
- **Kind.** Mistakes get a gentle "Not quite", never red flashing or guilt.
- **Movement explains.** Every animation shows what happened (you got it right, a lesson unlocked, XP was added). No movement just for show.

## 3. Letters (type)
- **Space Grotesk** for all words (weights 400, 500, 700). **JetBrains Mono** for numbers people read like data: MHz, XP, PCI, ARFCN, eNB IDs, counts like "5 / 12". Use tabular (same-width) digits.
- Both are free under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). They're **bundled inside the app** (npm `@fontsource/...` or the font files with their licence), never loaded from the internet. So the app looks the same offline, on Windows, and on a Mac or iPhone later.
- Sizes: 12 (small capital labels like "PICK ONE", letter spacing 0.12em) · 14 (small) · 16 (body) · 18 (answer tiles) · 23 (questions) · 28 (screen titles) · 40 (big numbers on the lesson-complete screen). Line height 1.25 for headings, 1.4 for text.

## 4. Spacing and shapes
- Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 px. Screen side margin 20 px.
- One content column, at most 440 px wide, centred. Wider windows show more background, not wider tiles (so a phone version later is the same layout).
- Corners: 14 px (tiles, buttons), 16–18 px (cards), round (pills, lesson circles).
- Smallest thing you can click: 44 × 44 px.
- The page always keeps the scrollbar's strip (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), even on a screen too short to scroll. *(Design review 1, 2026-11-04: without it, the whole frame jumped sideways by the scrollbar's width when you switched from the Path tab to a short tab.)*

**Window sizes (R22, 2026-11-05):** every screen works from a phone-narrow window to full screen.
- **Smallest window:** 360 × 560 (the page inside it; `MIN_SIZE` in `app/electron/main.cjs`). It opens at 480 × 760. On a big screen (1920 × 1080) the column stays 440 px wide in the middle, as above.
- **Nothing ever scrolls sideways.** On a narrow window things give way instead: the top bar's gaps shrink and its goal bar narrows (`clamp()`), and today's "+30" steps aside under 400 px (class `hud-wide-only` in `theme.css`; the lesson-complete screen still shows it). Beacon's bubble on lesson complete gets narrower and taller (at most half the window less 60 px), and "+N XP" shrinks (28 to 40 px) so it stays on one line.
- **A settings row never squeezes its words:** when they'd get narrower than 100 px, the control (a slider, a button, a choice) moves onto its own line under them, on the right.
- A click test checks every tab, Settings, the heatmap's sheet, the welcome, every question type and lesson complete at 360 × 560, and the column at 1920 × 1080 (`tests/sizes.spec.ts`).

**The keyboard everywhere (R22):**
- Everything you can click, you can reach with **Tab** and press with Enter or Space, and it shows a **2 px signal-blue ring** with a 2 px gap (`:focus-visible` in `theme.css`; switches and the slider draw it on their track or knob). The ring has at least 3:1 against every background a control sits on, in both looks (a unit test).
- **A focused control is never hidden:** the page scrolls it clear of the top bar and the bottom tabs (`scroll-padding: 64px 0 84px` while the app frame shows). Before R22, Tab parked the first lesson under the top bar.
- **Keys:** 1-9 pick an answer by its number; **A-I** pick a "Match the pairs" partner on the right (idea I41: the right column is lettered A, B, C in its dashed box until it's paired, then shows its partner's number); Enter checks or goes on; Esc leaves a lesson, closes a sheet or goes back to the path; on a picture you tap, the **arrow keys** move a small crosshair (5% of the picture a press, 1% with Shift) and Enter or Space taps there. The crosshair (a signal-blue ring with a dot, on a dark edge) shows only while the picture has the keyboard's focus. All of them are listed in Settings, Keys, "Show" (a sheet).
- A click test plays a whole lesson of every question type and the tap-the-picture lesson with the keyboard only, and walks Tab through every tab, the Keys sheet and the Day look; at every stop the ring must be there, not cut off by a box around it, and not under a bar (`tests/keyboard.spec.ts`, with a self-check that it does catch a missing or hidden ring).

**Never colour alone (R22):** right and wrong always have a tick or a cross as well as green or red: answer tiles, chips and the number box (since R09-R10), the **tap-the-picture dot** (now 24 px with a tick or a cross inside), and a **clue card** (its magnifier turns into a tick or a cross after Check). "Picked, not checked yet" has a shape too: the picked tile's **number box is filled in** (signal blue, dark number), like the left tile you're pairing in a match or your goal on the welcome. The heatmap has its bars and %, the path its ticks and locks, the goal its check.

## 5. Icons
- Our own icon set, drawn in code (SVG): 24 × 24 grid, 2 px lines, round ends, one colour (`currentColor`). One file (from R02).
- **No emoji in the app's own buttons and labels** (🔥 ⚡ 🔒 ⚙️ 🌡️ 🚗 📡 and the rest). Emoji look different on Windows, Mac and iPhone, so the app would change looks from one computer to the next. An automatic test checks the `.tsx` screen files. Lesson text keeps whatever the lesson files say.
- The basic set: flame, bolt, tower, lock, check, cross, waves, map pin, repeat, grid, gear, star, freeze, repair, back, close, sound on, sound off, info, chevron. *(Since added: search, image (R10); play, motion, gauge, target, moon, sun, backup, folder (R15, for the settings rows); keyboard (R22, the Keys row); restart (I59, "Again").)*
- **One icon, one meaning:** `repeat` (two arrows chasing round) means review and nothing else: the Review tab, the daily review, "Start the review", "Comeback". "Again" (play this lesson from the start) uses `restart` (one arrow going back round). A unit test in `app/src/icons.test.ts` checks it.

## 7. Movement (motion rules)
**What may move:** only position, size, turning and see-through-ness (transform and opacity), because those stay smooth on any computer (PRD 4e). Measured exceptions: drawing the path's line (`pathLength` or `stroke-dashoffset`) and counting numbers up. **Glows never animate a blur or shadow:** a glow is its own layer, and only its see-through-ness changes.

**Speed (measured in R23, 2026-11-05; checks in `app/tests/speed.spec.ts` and `app/src/r23.test.ts`):**
- **An endless loop goes on a page element** (a `span` or `div`), never on a part of a drawing (an SVG `<g>`, circle or path). On a page element the graphics chip runs a transform and see-through-ness loop by itself; inside a drawing, Chromium redraws it by code every frame. Beacon's breathing rings were inside its drawing and cost 48-61 ms of work a second (60 style passes) for as long as Beacon was on screen; on their own layer, under 7 ms. A one-off move (a hop, a blink, a pulse) may stay inside a drawing.
- **Nothing is a "fixed background"** (`background-attachment: fixed`): Chromium repaints the whole window at every step of a scroll for one. The night glow is its own fixed layer (`body::before`), painted once. Scrolling the 49-lesson path took about a fifth less work.
- **Sitting still costs almost nothing:** on any screen with nothing happening, under 25 ms of work a second and under 10 style passes a second (measured: under 7 and 5).
- **On a slow PC** (the computer slowed to a quarter speed in the test): every animation 30+ fps (measured 41-59), the path scrolls at 50+ fps.
- **Start-up:** the window shows within 1.5 s with the start screen (measured 0.2 s), the app's code has run and the save is read within 0.6-0.8 s (measured about 0.2 s), and the start screen waits only its half second.

**Speeds (one file, from R04: `app/src/theme/motion.tsx`; a test fails if another file types its own):**
| Kind | Time | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tap / hover | 120–180 ms, spring "snappy" (stiffness 500, damping 30) | A button squishes when pressed |
| Small feedback | 200–300 ms | A tile glows green; a wrong tile shakes 3 times |
| Screen change | 320–420 ms | A lesson slides up; going back slides it down |
| Pop-in | spring "bouncy" (stiffness 300, damping 14), 40–80 ms between items, at most 400 ms in total | Lesson circles appear one by one |
| Celebration | up to 1.6 s, never blocks a tap | Stars drop in, XP counts up, signal bars fill |
| Background ("ambient") | 2–4 s loops, very soft, at most 2 on screen, paused when the window is hidden | The current lesson's rings breathe; Beacon blinks |

**Moments that must feel great** (each gets checked with screenshots and the frame-rate test):
1. **Coming on air:** you finish a lesson, go back to the path, and its circle turns green with a ring burst; the next circle's lock pops off; the line between them draws itself.
2. **Right / not quite:** right = the tile glows green, Beacon's head rings pulse once; wrong = the tile shakes gently, Beacon looks worried; the bottom sheet slides up. *(Built in R11, 2026-11-04: the right tile, chip or number box pops 3.5% bigger and back while its glow layer flashes on and settles at 75%; a wrong one shakes 3 times, 6 → 4 → 2 px, in 0.3 s; the tap dot lands with a pop or a shake. No confetti after an answer (idea I31): the burst is for lesson complete only. The next question glides in from 40 px to the right as the old one leaves to the left (0.12 s out, 0.3 s in); the progress bar springs a touch past its new place (spring `step`, about 7% of one step) with a soft spark at its tip. In "put them in order" a chip glides into its slot and back, and the others glide to close the gap; a match tile's number box pops in when it gets its partner's number. All in `FEEDBACK` and `questionGlide` in the speed file, and `app/src/ui/feedback.tsx`. With Reduce motion: nothing moves, the looks change and the glow fades in; a new question fades in.)*
3. **Lesson complete:** Beacon cheers; stars drop in one at a time; XP counts up like a meter; signal bars fill one by one; a short "signal burst" (rings and wave pieces instead of plain confetti). *(Built in R12, 2026-11-04: the end screen has its own 112 px Beacon, which grows from its question size with a spring (one transform; idea I34) and cheers. Behind its head the signal burst: 3 rings (60 px, growing to 2.2 times and fading, 0.15 s apart) and 12 small wave arcs in the four bright colours, flying from 28 to 84 px out and fading, so they stay clear of the lesson's top row. The earned stars drop 28 px into dim outlined places, 0.2 s apart; XP counts up from 0 in a card ("XP EARNED", 40 px code font); the earned signal bars fill from the bottom, 0.1 s apart, in a second card ("SIGNAL"). All over by 1.4 s. One green full-width "Back to the path" (Enter), a quiet "Again" under it (I32), and "1 fixed" beside "2 of 3 right the first time" when a comeback was right (I43). The burst is drawn in the page, not on a canvas, so it shows on any computer; the confetti library is gone. Speeds: `COMPLETE` in the speed file; code: `app/src/LessonComplete.tsx`, `app/src/ui/SignalBurst.tsx`. With Reduce motion: no burst, nothing moves, the result is simply there.)* *(R18: the lesson's top row turns into its name, in green with a check, so the screen reads as "finished" (I46); the block sits in the middle of a tall window (I48); Beacon says a line beside its head; Esc goes back to the path keeping the result, like the main button.)*
4. **Screens:** the path opens scrolled to your current lesson; screens glide; nothing jumps.
5. **The top bar:** XP ticks up, the flame flickers once when the streak grows, the goal bar fills with a spring.

## 8. Beacon (the mascot)
- **Look:** a thin lattice tower (two legs, cross braces) with a round glowing head that has a face, and soft radio rings around the head. Drawn by Claude in code (SVG), animated with Motion (decision 2026-10-31: no Rive). Use `originX` / `originY` for turning parts (the T45 lesson).
- **Faces:** idle (rings breathe, blinks every 4–7 s), happy (^ ^ eyes, smile, green rings pulse), oops (round eyes, wobbly mouth, a little drop, rings dim), thinking (eyes up, dots), cheering (head bounces, extra rings), sleepy (eyes closed, "z", for late at night). Sizes: 48 px beside the current lesson on the path, about 58 px above questions, 96–140 px on the lesson-complete screen.
- **Built (R17, 2026-11-04):** `app/src/ui/Beacon.tsx`, a 100 × 136 drawing (head at 50, 40, so the rings are never cut off; look A's picture cut the top ring). The tower has its own two colour tokens (`beacon`, `beaconBrace`); the head sits on a short mast, so it stays joined to the tower when it hops. Loops: the rings breathe (the same `BREATHE` as the current lesson) and the eyes blink (idle, thinking, oops), so a screen with Beacon has room for no other loop. Happy sends one ring out; cheering hops three times, each smaller, and bursts two rings. `still` switches the loops off. Speeds: `BEACON` in the speed file.
- **Reacts (R18, 2026-11-05):**
  - **Path ("you are here"):** a 48 px Beacon stands beside your current lesson, on the side away from its name (the dotted line never runs there), feet level with the circle's bottom, just outside its inner ring. It sits inside the circle's pop-in, so it pops in with it; after you finish a lesson it arrives with the new current lesson and is happy for a moment (one ring pulse at 0.95 s, back to waiting at 2.6 s: `ON_AIR.beaconHappy` / `beaconRest`), before the top bar's turn. It blinks but its rings stay still (`breathe={false}`): the lesson's own rings breathe, so the path keeps 2 loops (idea I35). No speech bubble on the path: every spot near the circle is crossed by the line or a neighbour's name (idea I60).
  - **Questions:** waits (idle), happy after a right answer, a little worried after a miss, and thinking when a missed question comes back. It **speaks only** before the first question, after each answer and on a comeback (idea I39); the rest of the time the bubble fades out and the spot keeps its height. A right comeback says "fixed"; every 3rd right answer in a row says "on a roll" (`beaconMoment`).
  - **Lesson complete:** one line in a bubble beside the big Beacon's head (its corner just past the outer ring), popping in at 0.9 s once the burst's wave pieces are gone (`COMPLETE.says`). It goes with the stars ("perfect", "strong", "done"), or "you fixed the tricky ones" (`completeMoment`).
- **Rules:** reacts within 0.1 s of an answer; never covers words or buttons; never moves farther than its own width; speech bubbles of 8 words or fewer, plain and kind, never guilt (PRD 4d). Its lines live in one data file, so they're easy to change (`app/src/questionScreen.ts`, from R09; every line and when it's said, from R18).

## 9. Sound
- **Owner's choice (2026-11-04):** real sound effects from a free library, CC0 only ("no rights reserved"), credited anyway in a sounds credits file. If none can be reached from the cloud, code-made sounds stay until the owner downloads a pack (kenney.nl answered "403 blocked" from the cloud on 2026-11-04; opengameart.org answered).
- About 7 sounds: tap (very soft), right, not quite (gentle, not a buzzer), lesson complete (2 s at most), streak up, unlock, review start. Each file under 50 KB. Quiet by default (about 60%), and no sound before your first click (Windows rule for apps).
- The ⚙️ Sounds switch stays, with a "play a sample" button. *(Built in R15: "Play a sample" plays a right tone, then a not-quite one 0.4 s later, even with Sounds off (you asked for it). `playSample()` in `app/src/sound.ts`; R19 swaps the tones for real sounds behind the same call.)*
- **Built (R19, 2026-11-05):** seven real CC0 sounds in `app/src/sounds/`, credited in `app/public/licenses/sounds-CC0.txt`: six from Kenney's "Interface Sounds" and the review ping from Robin Lamb's pack (made from the public-domain VCSL and VSCO 2 CE libraries), both downloaded from OpenGameArt (kenney.nl itself is blocked from the cloud). They were picked by measuring them, not by ear (a cloud chat can't listen): tonal, short, and the right shape for the job.
  | Sound | When | What it is |
  |---|---|---|
  | `tap` | You pick a match tile or a chip (not a pick-one tile: its answer sound follows at once) | A tiny click, the softest sound |
  | `right` | A right answer | Four notes going up (G, D, G, D) in 0.29 s |
  | `notQuite` | A not-quite answer | Two soft notes going down (G to E), 0.33 s; pure tones, never a buzzer |
  | `complete` | Lesson complete, with the burst's first ring (`COMPLETE.sound`, 0.1 s) | A rising E major chord, 0.49 s |
  | `streak` | The streak grows, with the flame's flicker | A bright run of high notes, 0.54 s |
  | `unlock` | A new lesson comes on air, as its lock pops off (`ON_AIR.lockOff`, 0.85 s) | A smooth rising sweep, 0.38 s |
  | `review` | You start the daily review | A deep radar ping, about 1.2 s |
  Each file is under 34 KB. They are baked into the app's code (`?inline`), so they work offline and from the app's own files (fetch can't read `file://` pages). Every sound has its own level, so they all sound about as loud (about -16 dB before the volume; the tap about -25 dB). The ⚙️ Sound card has a **Volume** slider (0 to 100%, steps of 10, **60%** by default; you hear a right-answer sound at the new loudness when you let go) and "Play a sample" plays four in a row: tap, right, not quite, lesson done, 0.32 s apart, one ring each (idea I55). No sound before your first click or key: the sound system is set up when the app is idle after that first click (setting it up takes about 55 ms, so never inside the click). Reduce motion doesn't silence anything: sound isn't movement. If a sound can't be decoded, right and not quite fall back to the old code-made tones.

## 10. Never
- Copy anything from Duolingo (name, owl, colours, art, sounds) or any other app.
- Use AI-generated photos for identification questions (CLAUDE.md). The redesign changes how things look, never the facts or the lessons.
- Put emoji in the app's own buttons and labels, or load fonts and pictures from the internet.
- Animate blur, shadows, width or height in a loop; run more than 2 background loops on one screen.
- Put an endless loop on a part of a drawing (SVG), or give the page a fixed background (R23: both make the computer redraw by hand).
- Undo an earlier redesign chat's choice without a reason written down (a design review, or the owner's words).
